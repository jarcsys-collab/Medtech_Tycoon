## How the audio was made

- Every file was **synthesized from code** for this project: oscillators, FM electric piano, Karplus-Strong plucked strings, filtered noise drums, and an algorithmic reverb.
- There are **no samples, loops, sample packs, recordings, or presets** from any third party.
- All melodies, chord progressions, and arrangements are original. The main-menu melody was written for this game; the other melodies were generated by a seeded algorithm from common chord progressions, which are not copyrightable. No existing song is quoted or imitated.
- No AI music service or stock-music library was used, so there are no third-party terms of service attached.
